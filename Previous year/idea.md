## 💬 ANTICIPATED QUESTIONS & RESPONSES

### Question 1: "Why not more conservative given my risk aversion?"
**Response**: 
- State pension provides conservative base (€31k annually guaranteed)
- Portfolio acts as growth engine on top of safety net
- 65% equity allocation is moderate for 23-year horizon
- Conservative approach risks not meeting income goals

### Question 2: "What about German tax-advantaged pension products?"
**Response**:
- Rürup-Rente offers tax deductions but limited flexibility
- Direct investment provides more control and liquidity
- Tax optimization strategies achieve similar benefits
- Can layer on Rürup later if tax situation changes

### Question 3: "What if markets crash right before retirement?"
**Response**:
- Glide path reduces equity exposure as retirement approaches
- By age 65: Only 40% in equities vs. 65% today
- Sequence of returns risk managed through diversification
- Can extend working slightly if markets poor at 68

### Question 4: "How do you ensure I'll meet my income goals?"
**Response**:
- 85% success probability under base case assumptions
- Regular monitoring and adjustment process
- Flexibility to modify strategy based on progress
- Alternative scenarios available if assumptions change

### Question 5: "What are the costs and ongoing fees?"
**Response**:
- Total annual costs: <0.5% (ETF fees + platform costs)
- No advisory fees for passive implementation
- Tax optimization more than covers costs
- Transparent fee structure with no hidden charges
